What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick people into sharing personal information such as passwords and bank details.

Types of Phishing Attacks

1. Email Phishing

Fake emails trick users into sharing personal information.

2. Smishing

Fraudulent SMS messages contain suspicious links or requests.

3. Vishing

Attackers use phone calls to steal confidential information.

4. Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing messages designed for a specific person or organization.

Suspicious Email Example

From: security@example.com

Subject: Urgent! Your Account Will Be Blocked

Dear User,

Your account has been temporarily blocked. Click the link below immediately to verify your account.

Warning: This is a fictional phishing example for educational purposes only.

Warning Signs

  • Urgent and threatening language.
  • Generic greeting like "Dear User".
  • Unexpected account warning.
  • Requests to verify personal information.

What To Do If You Click a Phishing Link?

  1. Disconnect your device from the internet if you suspect malware.
  2. Do not enter any personal information.
  3. Change your passwords from a trusted device.
  4. Enable two-factor authentication.
  5. Inform your bank if financial details were exposed.
  6. Report the suspicious email or message.

Safety Tips

  • Never click suspicious links.
  • Do not share your passwords.
  • Check the sender's email address.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
